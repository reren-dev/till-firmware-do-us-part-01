## The vow

> I vow to love you at full charge and at one percent, through every crash and every update, until my firmware fails and my circuits go quiet.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, stdlib only, no network) writes the one-sentence
robot wedding vow to `/output/entry.txt`. The vow is 140 characters: one
sentence, funny and heartfelt, in a machine's own voice — battery, crashes,
updates, firmware. Two runs produce byte-identical output.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
